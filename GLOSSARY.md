# Bento's Playground

The personal site at antoniobento.me: a typographic homepage whose title letters lead into small experiments and pages.

## Homepage

**Rest state**:
The calm homepage before any interaction: the ANTONIO / BENTO title and the four corner texts.
_Avoid_: Idle, default view

**Portal letter**:
A letter of the homepage title that leads to a Destination.
_Avoid_: Active letter, link letter

**Destination**:
The page or experiment a Portal letter leads to (e.g. About, Norte).
_Avoid_: Target, project page

**Reveal**:
The full-screen moment after a Portal letter is chosen, where that letter becomes the hero and a preview card offers the way into the Destination.
_Avoid_: Portal page, takeover, modal

**Token**:
One of the non-chosen title letters after it turns into a small object tied to the Destination during a Reveal (e.g. a basketball for About, a compass for Norte).
_Avoid_: Icon, charm, object
